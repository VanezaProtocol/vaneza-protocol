// shielded trading · robinhood chain

trade.
without the trail

Vaneza is private trading engineered for Robinhood Chain. Capital arrives from whichever address you already hold, rests as sealed notes, and moves once proof made locally says it may. Open solvers fill orders. Public history keeps outcomes, never owners.

03 · route

Five hops, one idea

One deposit from the address you hold; after it, all motion occurs among sealed notes.

  1. afunding addresspublic
  2. bshield vaultkeeps deposits
  3. csealed notesowner hidden
  4. dbrowser proofmade locally

01

Deposit

Move funds into the vault from any address you hold. Sealed notes come back as your balance.

02

Prove

Local code in this tab demonstrates spending rights while revealing neither which note nor whose.

03

Settle

Solvers race to fill orders; the vault verifies each proof before value is released.

04

Exit

Withdrawals arrive back at your starting address. No detour, no fresh address to trust.

04 · limits

Honest edges

Less exposure, not none

Owners vanish from trade data anyone can read. That trades happen remains visible.

Habits still leak

Odd hours, round sums and reused routes can point back at you. Treat privacy as a practice.
